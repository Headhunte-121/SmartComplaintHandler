How to Annotate on Google Satellite

  • Move Vertices: Ensure Edit Vertices is active (or click the edit button on the left toolbar), then click on any road or walkway and drag the white circular handles.
  • Insert New Vertices: Click on the semi-transparent marker halfway along any segment to add a node.
  • Delete a Vertex: Right-click on any vertex circle to remove it.
  • Draw New Road / Path: Click Draw Road or Draw Footpath, click points on the satellite asphalt to trace the curb, then double-click to finish.
  • Snap: Geoman automatically snaps to nearby endpoints and corners so intersections connect cleanly.
  • Save & Export: Copy the code directly into frontend/src/data/campusTwin/campusGeometry.js.
